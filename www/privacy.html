Privacy policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026 · Operator: Ayush Pandey (Utkio)

1. Information We Collect

We collect only information necessary to deliver voice coaching: profile details (name, email, phone number, learning goals), session transcripts, and technical crash logs.

2. Microphone Access & Audio Data

The app accesses your microphone only when you explicitly tap the mic button during a live practice or scenario session. Audio is streamed directly over encrypted TLS/WSS protocols for real-time conversational processing. We do not listen to background audio or sell your voice data.

3. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Security

If you enter a personal third-party AI key, it is stored exclusively in private local storage on your device, is never transmitted to Utkio's application servers, and is permanently purged when you log out.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

To operate securely, we partner with enterprise service providers: Google Cloud AI (real-time voice/conversational processing), Supabase (encrypted database & auth), Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant payments), and Sentry (crash diagnostics).

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may request complete account and data deletion at any time in-app (Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing pandeyayush0811@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". All data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Contact & Grievance Redressal

For privacy inquiries or data rights requests: Grievance Officer Ayush Pandey · Email: pandeyayush0811@gmail.com · In-App Support: Settings → Contact the Co-Founder.
